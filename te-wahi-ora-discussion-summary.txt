TE WĀHI ORA WEBSITE — DISCUSSION SUMMARY
Date: 3 September 2026
Project: New WordPress site (not a rebuild of the old theme). Client supplies copy and photos; branding/logo handled elsewhere. SEO package declined.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. LIVE SITE (AUDIT)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Already WordPress (tewahiora.co.nz). Women-only charitable retreat at 129 Marine Parade North, Piha. Meals included. Optional counselling, massage, Reiki. Free monthly Check In (also called Check Up). Themed weekends. Donations, 400×$10, Wynsome Fund, vouchers, volunteers.

The public header has eight items: Home, Accommodation, Events, Extra Offerings, Retreat Rates, Support Us, Contact Us & Book, Bev’s Blog. Most of these have dropdowns (Home nests About Us and the story pages; Accommodation is a single link).

Problems: overlapping pages (rooms vs rates; many donate URLs; two Check In URLs; gift voucher listed under both Rates and Support); outdated events and years (20/25/35 years; 2015 vision; 2016–17 donate quotes); CHECK IN vs CHECK UP; two phone numbers (09 8128566 vs 09 812 8775); two emails; booking form missing rooms; no card payments; sitemap XML 500; homepage H1 is a phone number; long spiritual essays before practical facts; circular-icon calendar; Covid/cheque leftovers; crisis story on donate page.

Strengths to keep: women for women; “a place to belong to while moving into wholeness”; named rooms and GST prices; day stay $60; subsidy $140; meals as care; Check In timetable; Our Name story; volunteer quote; small capacity (about 6–8 for themed weekends).

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CLIENT BRIEF (PDF)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Positioning: charitable retreat, rest, reconnect, wholeness; Check In; workshops; extras; subsidies; group/facilitator use if values align. Audiences: women seeking rest/support; Check In/programme guests; donors/referrers/funders (NZ and overseas); aligned groups.

Goals: clarity; purpose/values; easier stay/pricing/subsidy/events/Check In/enquiry; much stronger donate path (one-off, recurring, international); simpler for staff to update; still feel like Te Wāhi Ora (warm, welcoming, safe, history). Form checkbox “lead generation” really means enquiries + donations, not a marketing funnel.

Proposed nav (starting point only): Home | Stay | What We Offer | About | Support Us | Contact.
Function to discuss: complex forms, events calendar/tickets, online booking (later). Want: donate UX, Mailchimp, routed enquiries. Copy and images from them. Inspiration: Women’s Healing Sanctuary (AU) — pathways, not their look. Brief uses macron: Te Wāhi Ora; live site often does not.

Gaps in brief: Bev vs public voice; Check In naming; crisis-service boundary; donor recognition format; who updates CMS; photos; practical booking process.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. FEEL (AGREED DIRECTION)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Not: heavy Māori visual identity (name story kept, not the theme); not two moods (no “cool despair / get out of my life”); not a spa or generic wellness; not a pulpit of spiritual essays on the homepage.

Yes: the site should feel a little like going there — house AND people AND nature. Three feeling words to test with the client: SAFE (explore yourself, safe to share), COMMUNITY (women helping women), GROWTH (develop). Healing matters and should appear on the home page, but not as the first thing that grabs attention.

Open wording debate (for client email):
- Safety can sound like a refuge; alternatives: welcome, ease.
- Community vs connection (connection = sharing; community = also donors/volunteers).
- Growth can sound corporate/self-help; wholeness is already their word (tagline/name).
- Rest and place (Piha) are important but better as how the feeling is delivered, not a fourth slogan.
- Suggested sets to offer them: (1) safety, community, growth (2) safety, connection, wholeness (3) rest, belonging, growth.

“Porch not pulpit” meant: invite and make the next step clear, don’t preach first. Drop the metaphor; use the triad instead.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. USER GROUPS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(1) Women booking, including price and sponsored options
(2) People giving time or money
(3) Gifters — close to (1)
(4) People wanting to use the facilities

Discussion: gifters are the same product as stays but a different user (often not the guest); also overlap with giving (pay it forward). Group hire is real but small — don’t equal-weight in nav. Missing if only four: Check In as a first visit that is not a booked night; people referring someone else; staff using WordPress.

Mentally: guests (stay + Check In), supporters (money/time), gifters (bridge), groups (quiet), staff (CMS). Only the first two shout.
