## How to read these diagrams

The last version looked like three flavours of Home / About / Support / Contact. These three are different **jobs for the website**, not different labels on the same tree.

### Header vs dropdown vs one long page

In the lists below:

- **Bold names in the “Header” line** = items in the top bar.
- **Book** and **Donate** = buttons (always visible), not extra menu titles. They jump to a form or a donate page.
- **Indented names** can be *either*:
  1. **Dropdown links** (each is its own short page), or
  2. **In-page sections** (one long page with a sticky mini-menu: Rooms, Prices, Food…).

That choice is marked for each sitemap as **many short pages** or **few long pages**.

### When fewer long pages are better

- The visitor is doing **one job** (understand a stay, RSVP to Check In, give money).
- They need to **compare** (rooms next to prices).
- They are on a **phone** and extra clicks cost them.
- Staff will not update that content often.

**Check In + RSVP** belong on **one page**: dates and timetable above, form below. Two pages made RSVP feel like a separate product.

**Rooms + prices** belong on **one page** for the same reason (Sitemap 1 and 2 do this; 3 can keep them split if Stay is not the main story).

### When more short pages are better

- Content is **long or personal** (Our Beliefs, Vision, Bev’s letters) — dumping them all on About would be a wall of text.
- Content **changes often** (each retreat weekend) — easier as separate posts.
- Different **audiences** (bequest vs volunteer vs $10/month) if each has a campaign — but they can still share one Donate hub with sections.

### Booking the place

Two different actions, both missing from the last diagrams:

1. **Book a stay** — a woman books a room (header button → form).
2. **Book the location** — a group or facilitator hires the house (brief: aligned group/retreat use). That is its own path, not buried as “contact.”

### Footer (all three sitemaps)

Not a sitemap item, but always there:

- Address, one phone, emails (bookings vs general)
- **Book a stay** and **Donate** again
- Next Check In date (short link)
- Newsletter
- Cancellation, Do you need help?, [Links]
- Social (Facebook, Instagram)
- [Bev’s Blog] and [Many Paths Many Voices] can live here so they do not steal a header slot

## Sitemap 1 — Guest product site

**Approach:** Behaves like a small retreat you can actually book. Header is the stay and the diary. Donate is a button, not a matching menu item.

**Pages:** Mix — **one long Stay page** (rooms, prices, food, extras, Piha, gift, groups, cancellation as sections). **Short pages** for About essays and each event. **Check In = one page** (info + RSVP form).

**Header:** Stay · What’s on · About · Support us · **[Book a stay]** · **[Donate]**

**Home** (separate page, not a dropdown)

1. Hero: house / beach / women — tagline. Buttons: **Book a stay** | See rooms | Free Check In
2. What this is, in a few lines (women, Piha, meals included)
3. Healing / rest / community — **not** the first thing, a second band
4. Rooms from $X, meals in, subsidy mentioned in one line → Stay
5. Next Check In (date + “free, RSVP”) → Check In page
6. This month’s retreat weekend (if any)
7. Gift a stay
8. Support strip: 400×$10 + Wynsome → Donate
9. One guest quote → Testimonials

**Stay** — one page, in-page links: Rooms · Prices · Food · While you’re here · Piha · Gift · Groups · Practical

- Rooms + prices *(Accommodation + Retreat Rates merged)*
- Food *(Nourishment)*
- Extra offerings
- About Piha
- Gift a stay *(one voucher page, also linked from Donate)*
- **Book the location / bring a group** *(new heading using existing contact/enquiry; group hire from the brief)*
- Cancellation policy
- Button at top and bottom: **Book this stay** → booking form (form can sit at the bottom of Stay **or** on `/book` — one form, two entry points)

**What’s on**

- Check In *(merged Check In URLs + RSVP form on the same page)*
- Calendar + event list
  - Each event page *(Grief, Writing, Nature, Moving On, Finances, [Sewing], [Surfing], [Exploring Our Roots], …)*

**About** — short pages (dropdown)

- About us
- Our name
- Our ethos
- Our beliefs
- Our vision
- Our guests / patrons
- Testimonials *(+ [I go alone])*

**Support us** — one Donate hub with sections, plus extra pages only where the story is long

- How to give *(Donate + How to donate + Donation information + Give a Little, merged)*
- 400 × $10
- Wynsome Fund
- Volunteer + donated time *(can be one “Give time” page)*
- Bequests
- Thanking our supporters · [Project Dishwasher]

**Not in the header:** Contact (footer + Book button). Newsletter in footer. [Bev’s Blog] in footer.

## Sitemap 2 — Diary first (community calendar)

**Approach:** The site is a **noticeboard**. The first question is “what’s happening?” Stay is one of the ways to be here, not the centre. Suits Check In and workshops. Unusual for a lodging site; very different from Sitemap 1.

**Pages:** **Short.** Almost everything is a dated card. Home is a feed. Stay is a compact page. About is a single long page with anchors (essays as sections or “read more” drawers).

**Header:** This month · Check In · Stay · About · **[RSVP / Book]** · **[Donate]**

The main button **changes with context**: on Check In it says RSVP; on Stay it says Book a stay; on Home it can split into two small buttons.

**Home** = the diary

1. Next date, huge: “Check In, Sat 26 Sep” or “Writing weekend 16–18 Oct”
2. Buttons: **RSVP** or **Book that weekend**
3. Strip of upcoming cards (Check In + retreats)
4. Quiet line: “Or come any time for a rest stay” → Stay
5. One photo of the house
6. Donate / volunteer line at the bottom
7. Healing/community in a short FAQ: who it’s for, not a sermon

**This month / What’s on** (the real “front section”)

- Calendar (all events as cards, not a prose year)
- Each event page
- [Old events] still listed as past cards or an archive

**Check In** — one page: what it is, timetable, dates, **RSVP form at the bottom**

**Stay** — shorter than Sitemap 1; still rooms+prices together

- Rooms and prices
- Food and extras (can be one “What’s included” section)
- About Piha
- Gift a stay
- **Hire the house** (groups/facilitators) — enquire form
- Cancellation
- **Book a stay** form

**About** — **one long page** with in-page titles: About us, Name, Ethos, Beliefs, Vision, Patrons, Testimonials. [Links] at the end.

**Donate** — not a big header word; the **Donate button** goes to one page: methods, 400×$10, Wynsome, bequests, Give a Little, thank-you, [dishwasher], volunteer/time as a tab or section.

**Footer:** full contact, newsletter, Do you need help?, blogs.

**Why this is different:** A donor or a tired woman who only wants a quiet Tuesday still *starts* from the calendar. Support/About are not equal pillars. Booking the location for a group is under Stay as “Hire the house.”

## Sitemap 3 — Two sites in one header (Come / Help)

**Approach:** Split the audience in half, like two front doors on the same house.

- **Come** = every way a woman (or a group) **uses** the place.
- **Help** = every way someone **keeps it going**.

No About in the top bar. Story is inside Come (so guests still find it) and repeated inside Help (so funders still find it). Extreme, but it stops Contact/About/Support looking like every other charity site.

**Pages:** **Two long hubs** (Come, Help) + **short event posts** + **one Check In page** + **one Book page** + **one Group enquiry page**.

**Header:** Come · Help · **[Book a stay]** · **[Give]**

**Home** — only a sorter, very short

1. Photo of Piha / the house
2. Two equal doors: **I want to come** | **I want to help**
3. Tiny third line: “Facilitators: you can book the house” → Group enquiry
4. Healing appears only as a sentence under the doors
5. No prices on Home (that’s Come). No bank details on Home (that’s Help).

**Come** — one long hub, in-page: Stay · Check In · Events · The place · Our story · Practical

- Stay: rooms+prices, food, extras, gift, cancellation
- Check In: full content + **RSVP on this page** (or jump to `#rsvp`)
- Events: calendar + links to short event posts
- About Piha
- Our story: About us, name, ethos, beliefs, vision, patrons, testimonials [I go alone]
- Practical: getting here, Do you need help?, newsletter
- **Book the location:** “Groups and facilitators” section + form (hire)
- Persistent **Book a stay** button (header) → dedicated book form (stay enquiry only)

**Help** — one long hub, in-page: Money · Time · Gifts · Thanks · Story for funders

- How to donate (all methods + Give a Little)
- 400 × $10
- Wynsome
- Bequests
- Gift a stay *(same voucher page as Come)*
- Volunteer + donated time
- Thanking supporters · [Project Dishwasher]
- Short “why this exists” (can reuse About us, not all seven essays)
- **Give** button → top of this page or a simple donate widget

**Not a header item:** Contact — footer only, except Book and Group forms. [Bev’s Blog] in footer.

**Why this is different:** You cannot browse “a normal About page” without choosing guest vs supporter. Booking a **room** and booking the **location** are both under Come, as two forms. Donate is not sitting next to About like a standard NFP template.

## Home, buttons, footer — quick rules for all

- **Home** must answer: what is this, who is it for, what happens next. Healing is on Home, below the first actions.
- **[Book a stay]** is always in the header (and footer). It is how booking shows up; it should not be a menu item you hunt for.
- **[Donate / Give]** is always in the header (and footer).
- **Book the location** (groups) is a section + form, not a seventh header word.
- **Footer** carries contact so Contact does not have to copy About and Support in every sitemap.
