Sitemap pack

A draft from Hannah. There are two sitemap options. They are meant to be compared, not both built. You can pick one, or mix pieces (for example B’s navigation with A’s writing pages).

How to use this pack: Scroll to a tree. Each box is a page. Click a box to open a designed preview of that page (placeholder photos and lorem where copy is still to come). Use the preview’s own navigation as a guest would. Then come back here.

Sitemap A is our proposed labels: Stay, What we offer, From the house. Light header, clay colour, square corners. Writing (newsletter, Bev’s blog, Many Paths, Many Voices) has its own section, with a short hub and a grid of all posts.

Sitemap B uses the navigation they suggested: Retreats & Stays, Events, About, Support us, Contact, Book. Dark teal, rounder shapes, photo page titles. Newsletter, Bev’s blog and Many Paths sit on About.

Every themed weekend uses the same event page: what it is, who it is for, what happens, when, place, cost, book. Both options are meant to be built in WordPress (pages, posts, forms) — the previews are clickable stand-ins, not the live site.

Bookings and money are not decided on the trees. That sits in a separate note: Booking and payment system proposal.
