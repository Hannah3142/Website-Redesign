Te Wāhi Ora Women’s Retreat

Booking and payment system proposal

Draft · September 2026

This is just a draft from me (Hannah), but because I am not an expert please ask Harper Digital for their professional advice before making any decisions. Prices below are ballparks for September 2026 — confirm with each vendor.

The first draft aimed at a system the Trust does not pay to keep running. There is no monthly Humanitix fee. Stay option A also has no software fee. Stay option B is the exception: the Trust would pay for a live calendar.

When someone pays, they can pay online (card or Humanitix) and cover the platform fees themselves, so Te Wāhi Ora still receives the listed price. Or they can transfer to the Trust’s bank account. Bank transfer has no extra fees for anyone. Staff still mark those bookings as paid.

Events and Check Ins use Humanitix (charity rate), embedded on the website. Humanitix is only for the programme or the free RSVP — not for beds. The guest pays Humanitix’s small fee on paid tickets; the Trust still keeps the ticket price. Free Check In RSVPs cost nothing. Humanitix giving its own profits to other charities does not change what Te Wāhi Ora receives, so it is not a money decision for the Trust.

Every bed (rest stay, Check In overnight, or staying for an event) goes through one stay system, at the same rates — from $180 a night including meals, or $60 for a day stay. Choose A (form and emails, no software fee) or B (live calendar, the Trust pays for that software).

Hire of the house stays an enquiry. If it fits, they pay by bank transfer. Subsidies are decided by staff, not paid online.

Still to decide

  • Stay A (same bed work as now, more professional for the guest) or stay B (less diary work, Trust pays for the calendar).
  • How long to hold an unpaid bank-transfer booking (for example five days), so beds are not blocked forever.

Would switching be good or bad?

Switching means leaving “email us / fill the form / bank transfer only” for this proposal (Humanitix for events and Check In, plus stay A or B for beds).

Good

  • Looks more professional for the woman booking: cards, an embedded RSVP or ticket, no cheque, less “email us to see if we have a room.”
  • The Trust can still be paid the listed price (she covers card or Humanitix fees, or she uses bank transfer with no extra fees).
  • Check In and paid events get a real attendee list, instead of a pile of emails.
  • Every bed still sits in one stay system — that part does not get messier.
  • Stay B is the version that actually cuts diary work on ordinary nights.

Bad

  • Stay A is not less work for the house. Beds stay a form, a diary and emails. Humanitix is a new login. Paid events take more setup than a contact page.
  • Someone has to learn and keep two tools (Humanitix plus the stay form or calendar).
  • A woman who wants the programme and a bed still books twice — same as Check In now.
  • Stay B costs money every month. A does not, but then you mostly bought a nicer front, not a lighter week.
  • Online payers pay a small platform fee. Bank transfer stays the no-fee path.

Worth switching if they want the site to take money and RSVPs like a current retreat, or if they will pay for B to stop chasing the diary. Not worth switching if the only aim is less admin and they would pick A — that is the same job with extra software.
